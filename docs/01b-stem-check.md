# GrainGuard STEM check

Verdict: GrainGuard's working principle (reading the grain's temperature and equilibrium humidity on a cable, and running the fan only when the plenum air's equilibrium moisture content will cool or dry the grain) sits within known physics, chemistry and biology. Of the 33 claims for option A, the design of record, **19 are within known science, 12 are plausible and wait for TRL 4 data or a purchase check, and 2 fail with a fix proposed to Amish**. Seven details failed and were fixed under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 (GGD-DEC-001 D10, D12 to D17): the battery's hydrogen had no way out of the sealed box (S18), the mast tube was checked at 235 MPa although its grade is S195T and the mast alone could not carry the 20 W panel with the stay off (S23, S24), nothing said which side of the bin the mast stands on (S15), a fixed 125 kHz LoRa channel is not a legal mode under 47 CFR 15.247 (S20), the plenum probe is unrated below -10 °C (S31, now zeroed against the ambient sensor) and a short in the thin bus cable was protected only by the 5 A battery fuse (S32). Two failures go to Amish because their fixes change settings he decided on 2026-10-02: the battery's electrolyte freezes near -20 °C at the 50 % disconnect, above the -30 °C the design must survive (S17, open item 11), and with a lead-acid charge efficiency added, the 20 W panel refills the battery in 3.5 winter days, not 3 (S14, open item 12). `stem_check` is **open**. Method: STANDARDS section 20. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, which imports the calculation note GGD-CAL-001 (`docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`).

## Fixes proposed to Amish

Two rows are **Fails, fix proposed** and go to Amish rather than being fixed here, because each fix changes a setting he decided on 2026-10-02.

- **S17, battery freezing (GGD-DEC-001 open item 11).** By the BCI freezing table the electrolyte freezes near -20 °C at 50 % charge, near -30 °C at 65 % and near -36 °C at 70 %. The usable energy at those settings gives 7.7, 5.4 and 4.6 days with no sun if the 60 % cold derating of section D applies, or 12.8, 9.0 and 7.7 days if it does not at the station's very low draw (about C/600). The recommendation is the 70 % figure: it leaves a 6 K margin below -30 °C, and its autonomy is met on the low-draw basis and short on the conservative one, which a cold-chamber test at TRL 4 settles. An empty battery stops the fan (the safe state); a frozen one cracks.
- **S14, winter recovery (GGD-DEC-001 open item 12).** With a lead-acid charge efficiency of 85 %, which the 60 % derating did not include, the 20 W panel refills from half charge in 3.5 days at 1.5 peak sun hours; about 23 W is needed for 3 days. The recommendation is a 25 W panel (2.7 days), with the panel bracket and the mast checks rerun for it (the stay-off service limit would fall to about 27 m/s); the alternative is to restate the target as 3.5 days.
