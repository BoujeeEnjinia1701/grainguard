# BOM notes

- All costs are indicative USD retail prices for one prototype in 2026. They are not quotes. Supplier types are given where no single supplier is chosen.
- Item numbers 1 to 11 and 14 match the callouts in `media/exploded.png` and drawing GGD-DWG-001; item 15 (antenna) has a callout too. Item 2 is split into 2a (two pods with SHT45) and 2b (four pods with SHT40). Items 12, 13, 16 and 17 have no callout.
- Value-engineering target: USD 260 per bin (`budget_usd`, a hypothetical control target, not a limit; Amish, 2026-10-01). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 410.50 per bin, items 1 to 11 and 13 to 17 (USD 150.50 over the target). The concept kit was USD 256.50; the design for construction (GGD-DDR-003) added USD 133.00, the 2026-10-02 decisions USD 17.00 and the two battery vents of GGD-DEC-001 D10 USD 4.00 (an estimate), mainly the mast weldment, stay and six clamps (line 10), the pod glands and stops, and lines 15 to 17, which the concept lacked. The farmhouse receiver (item 12, USD 22.00) is costed once per farm, as decided in GGD-DDR-001 (D1). A first prototype with one bin and its receiver costs USD 432.50. `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` reads this file and prints these totals (GGD-CAL-001, section H). Cost drivers and savings worth trying: GGD-DEC-001, Value engineering.
- Item 10's mast weldment needs a welder or a local fabricator; the welding labour is not costed.
- Item 14, the plenum temperature probe ($9.50), measures the fan's heat so the decision rule does not rely on an assumed value (R4, GGD-CAL-001 section B).
- The bin, grain, aeration fan, fan starter and concrete pad already exist on the farm and are not costed.
- Item 11 is the only part connected to mains and must be installed by a licensed electrician.

Decided by Amish on 2026-10-02 (GGD-DEC-001) and now in `bom.csv`: a 20 W panel in line 8 (USD 34.00, USD 9.00 more; the 430 mm wide bracket is part of the line), conditional on the stay-off mast check, which holds only to a 30 m/s gust (open decision 9); a charge controller with an adjustable low-voltage disconnect in line 7 (USD 18.00, USD 8.00 more, an allowance, no quote); conformal coating in line 13 stays the phosphine protection for the prototype (item 5). Both new prices are indicative estimates to confirm when parts are bought.

Kit 1.8 and 1.9 upgrade, 2026-10-24 (GGD-DDR-005): line 4 now carries two M16 labyrinth vents in place of the M12 membrane breather (USD 20.00 to USD 24.00, an estimate; D10); line 5 and line 12 are set to SF10 with a 500 kHz bandwidth, the legal mode in the US band (D15, no price change); line 16 adds a 0.5 A fuse on the bus cable's 12 V feed (D17, within its price); line 10's mast tube is EN 10255 medium series, grade S195T or better (D12). Item numbers in the D3 model (`cad/src/ggd_detail.py`) match these lines for every made and bought part, so each STEP file in `cad/step/A/` belongs to a BOM line. Two proposals with Amish would change prices: a 25 W panel in line 8 (open item 12, a few dollars more, no quote) and the disconnect setting of line 7 (open item 11, no price change).
